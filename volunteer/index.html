Why Volunteer?

Volunteering with us is about making a real difference for children in need while enriching your own life. Your time helps build hope, trust, and opportunity for their futures, while also giving you a chance to connect, grow, and witness the power of giving firsthand. Together, we’re transforming lives—both theirs and ours.

Be A Volunteer

Teaching & Mentoring

Help with homework, teach basic subjects, or mentor children to build skills and confidence.

Event planning & Coordination

Organize fundraising events, community outreach, or holiday celebrations for the children.

Childcare Support

Engage with children in arts, sports, and group play, creating a positive environment.

Administrative support

Assist with office tasks such as data entry, organizing records, and managing communication channels.

Help raise awareness for our cause by creating content, managing social media accounts, or assisting with marketing campaigns.

Fundraising & Donor relations

Help in raising funds, writing grant proposals, or building relationships with donors to support the orphanage’s ongoing work.

Opportunities For Volunteering

We offer a variety of volunteer roles to match your skills and interests. Whether you’re passionate about working directly with children or supporting behind-the-scenes operations, there’s a place for you to make an impact.

No matter your background or expertise, your contribution will be valued and make a real difference in the lives of the children we serve. If you’re ready to get involved, we’ll help match you with a role that suits your strengths!

Frequently Asked Questions

Curious about how we work or how you can help? Here are answers to the most common questions we get. If you still need help, don’t hesitate to reach out!

1 : Who can volunteer?

Anyone with a passion for helping children and supporting our mission is welcome to volunteer. We value dedication and a willingness to contribute.

2 : Do I need any specific skills or qualifications to volunteer?

Most roles don’t require specific skills, just a positive attitude and enthusiasm. However, certain tasks (e.g., teaching or counseling) may require specific expertise.

3: Is there an age requirement to volunteer?

Volunteers must be at least 18 years old. Those under 18 may participate with parental consent and supervision, depending on the activity.

4. How much time do I need to commit as a volunteer?

The time commitment is flexible and depends on the role. It can range from a few hours a week to a full day, depending on your availability.

5. Can I volunteer on weekends or during holidays?

Yes, we welcome volunteers on weekends and during holidays, as these are often the times when extra help is needed.

6. Is there a minimum or maximum duration for volunteering?

There’s no set limit! You can volunteer for a single event or on a recurring basis. Long-term commitments are especially appreciated.

7. How do I sign up to become a volunteer?

Fill out our volunteer application form on the website, and we’ll get in touch with you to discuss the next steps.

8. Do I need to complete a background check?

Yes, all volunteers working directly with children must complete a background check for the safety of our beneficiaries.

9. Will there be an interview or orientation before I start volunteering?

Yes, we provide a brief interview and orientation to help you understand our mission, your role, and how you can make the greatest impact.

10. What kind of tasks or activities will I be doing as a volunteer?

Activities range from teaching and mentoring to organizing events, fundraising, administrative tasks, and assisting with daily operations.

11. Can I choose the area I want to volunteer in?

Absolutely! We’ll work with you to match your interests and skills to the areas where we need support.

12. Will I be working directly with the children?

Yes, some roles involve direct interaction with the children, while others focus on behind-the-scenes support. We’ll guide you based on your preferences and skills.

13. Where is the orphanage located?

Our orphanage is located at Kawempe A, Magajo, Nansana,
Wakiso, Uganda. Detailed directions have been provided on the contact page. Please, check the google maps or feel free to call us via those contacts.

14. Do you provide transportation or accommodations for volunteers?

We currently don’t provide transportation or accommodations, but we can assist with recommendations and planning.

15. Will training or support be provided during my volunteer period?

Yes, we provide training, resources, and on-site support to ensure you’re comfortable and effective in your role.

16. What are the rules and policies for volunteers?

Volunteers are expected to maintain professionalism, respect privacy, and follow guidelines shared during orientation. A detailed handbook will be provided.

17. What should I do if I need to cancel or reschedule my volunteer shift?

Please inform us as soon as possible so we can make alternative arrangements. Communication is key.

18. Can groups or organizations volunteer together?

Yes, we welcome groups and organizations! Contact us to plan activities tailored to your group’s size and interests.

19. How does my volunteering help the children and the orphanage?

Your time and skills provide essential support, from mentorship and education to logistical and emotional assistance, directly impacting the children’s lives.

20. Can I continue supporting the orphanage after my volunteer period ends?

Absolutely! You can continue to support us by donating, spreading the word, or participating in future events.
