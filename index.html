Welcome to Wings of Care Foundation

Our story is simple: we’re here to give children the support they need to thrive. From providing a safe, loving home to ensuring access to education, we’re building a foundation for brighter tomorrows. And the best part? None of this happens without people like you.

Vision

To create an Empowered, Transformed and sustainable communities by 2030.

Mission

Our mission is to provide love, care and support to the needy through holistic programs and out- reach initiatives. We strive to empower them with education basic necessities and a sense of belonging

See The Difference You're making

When you support us, you’re changing lives in ways that matter. Kids get healthy meals, access to education, and a safe place to grow and dream. You’re not just helping today—you’re creating a future filled with hope and possibility.

Community Services

Clean water is life—every sip promotes health, prevents disease, and offers hope for a brighter, healthier future.

Education

Learning opens doors to brighter futures, empowering every child with knowledge, skills, and opportunities to succeed.

Healthy Food

Every child deserves nutritious meals to grow, learn, and thrive, building strong bodies and healthy minds for a brighter future.

Medical Care

Access to healthcare ensures every child stays safe and healthy by providing vital vaccinations, regular check-ups, and timely care for their overall well-being.
