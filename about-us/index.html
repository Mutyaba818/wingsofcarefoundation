About Wings Of Care Foundation

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to provide love, care and support to the needy through holistic programs and out-reach initiatives. We strive to empower them with education basic necessities and a sense of belonging.

Our Vision is to create an Empowered, Transformed and sustainable communities by 2030.

Our Background

Our ministry is based in Kawempe, one of the surburbs of Kampala city and we are a committed team aimed at supporting the children, the youth, and other vulnerable groups with basic essential necessities and we also have the intention of setting up hands on skilling projects to equip them with skills that can help them to sustain themselves.

We do dis-ministerial work in conjunction with our local parish and this has been on since 2022.

Our main objective came up as a result of the poverty that cropped in as an after effect of covid-19 which left many people around our Area jobless and could not sustain the children.

It is important to note that some of these children were totally abandoned by their families and we have our small home where we provide safe shelter for them and the rest of the help comes from their families.

Our Objectives

  • To provide daily meals for children in our neighbor-hood whose families experience food scarcity.
  • To improve their standards of living through setting up a skill development (vocational training) center to equip them with skills that are to help them to be job creators to sustain themselves.
  • To organize out-reach programs to involve moral canceling and motivational talks.
  • To create a safe and supportive environment for needy kids and other vulnerable persons
